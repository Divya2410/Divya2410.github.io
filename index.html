Research

My research focuses on understanding the diversity of Type Ia Supernovae (SNe Ia) and the physical mechanisms that drive their observed properties, with the broader goal of improving their reliability as cosmological distance indicators. I am studying the diversity of SNe Ia using photometry, spectroscopy, and spectropolarimetry, with particular interest in connecting their observed spectral and polarization signatures to the underlying explosion physics and progenitor systems. I am also interested in studying the origin, structure, and evolution of magnetic fields in galaxies. Using optical polarimetry, I aim to probe the large-scale magnetic field structure of nearby galaxies and investigate its connection to star formation, interstellar dust, and the dynamical processes shaping galaxies.
